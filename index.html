Rooted in patience

What is Evergreen?

EVERGREEN is your place to discover and enjoy the world of bonsai. Whether you're just starting out or already passionate about growing bonsai, our platform brings everything together in one place from learning about different bonsai varieties and proper care to finding products and professional services.

Our goal is to make bonsai more accessible and inspire more people to appreciate the patience, creativity, and beauty that goes into growing every tree.

Plant of the Day

Begin your connection with bonsai that are naturally resilient, highly responsive, and full of historical character.

Jade Plant

Beginner

Perfect for beginners. Thick, fleshy leaves, woody reddish-brown stems, and exceptional drought tolerance make it an ideal indoor bonsai.

Chinese Elm

Intermediate

Perfect for beginners. Fast growing, beautifully delicate small leaves, and highly responsive to pruning.

Japanese Black Pine

Advanced

The undisputed king of coniferous bonsai. Resilient needles, rugged bark, and classic stark structural potential.
